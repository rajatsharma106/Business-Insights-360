# Business-Insights-360
This project focuses on analyzing sales and financial data of a hardware company to generate meaningful insights and support business decision-making. The analysis includes customer performance, market performance, and financial reporting.


**Tools Used**

•	SQL

•	Power BI Desktop

•	Excel

•	DAX language

•	DAX studio (for optimizing the report)

**Company’s background**

AltiQ hardware is a company which has grown vastly in the recent years, and opened business all over the globe. It is a company which sells, computer and computer accessories through three mediums/channel

•	Retailers

•	Direct

•	Distributors

Recently the company has faced a unforeseen loss by opening store in America based on the surveys, intuition and some excel analysis and also the company’s competitors has handful of analytics team to perform analysis and make data driven decision. So, the AltiQ hardware has no other option other than building their analytics team for data driven insights and decisions in the future to survive better in the industry.
